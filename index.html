How is my match calculated?

Every UB program gets a score out of 100, built from three parts:
Academic fit 60%Interests 30%Career goal 10%
  1. Academic fit. Your subjects are grouped into areas (math, computing, physics…). Each program weighs the areas it needs most — for example Computer Science leans on computing and math.
  2. Interests. How many of your chosen interests match what the program is about.
  3. Career goal. A bonus when your goal points directly to that program.
If a key subject is missing from your results, the advisor uses a cautious estimate and flags it under Watch out for. With Gemini connected, your results are sent to Google to generate the reasoning; otherwise everything stays in your browser.
